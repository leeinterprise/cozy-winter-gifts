Not medical advice. If you think you have seasonal affective disorder (SAD) or depression, talk to a doctor or mental-health professional before starting light therapy, especially if you have eye conditions, bipolar disorder, or take light-sensitizing medication.

What to look for

  • 10,000 lux: the brightness level commonly cited for light therapy. Check at what distance the maker rates it.
  • UV-filtered: you want bright visible light, not UV.
  • Size: larger panels give you more freedom to move while sitting in front of them.
  • Adjustable brightness and timer: handy for building a morning routine.

Many people use the lamp in the morning, positioned off to the side while having breakfast or working. Follow the manufacturer's directions and your clinician's advice.
