How to choose

  • Check for a UL or ETL safety listing and an auto shut-off timer.
  • Throw size fits one person on a couch; for beds look at twin and up.
  • Confirm the controller detaches so it can be machine washed.

Quick answers

Is a heated throw safe to leave on overnight?

Follow the manufacturer's instructions. Many throws have an auto shut-off timer; look for UL or ETL certification and don't fold or bunch the blanket while it's on.

Can you wash a heated throw?

Most electric throws are machine washable once the controller is unplugged and detached. Check the care label first.
